Asha's Remembrance

---

GEMMA 2 · 2B

---

The kitchen’s pressure cooker echoed with a small argument. She squeezed lime into the dal. A blue tin of sewing buttons sat on the counter, smelling of thread and Vicks.

The song filled the air every Sunday. The old transistor played Rabindra Sangeet, a little too loud. She sang off-key, daring anyone to correct her. As dusk painted the terrace in shades of orange and purple, she watered the tulsi and complained about pigeons while the neighbourhood went dark one window at a time.

"Ei to shob — this is all there is." She said it when the power went out, when someone cried, when the mango was too sour.
